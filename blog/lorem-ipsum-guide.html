Lorem ipsum: origin, meaning, and the best free generators in 2026

June 8, 2026 4 min read By WrdKit Team

You've seen it a thousand times in mockups, wireframes, and design templates. But where did lorem ipsum actually come from? What does it mean? And why, 500+ years later, is it still the default placeholder text for virtually every designer on the planet? Here's the full story.

The standard lorem ipsum text

Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit, sed do eiusmod tempor incididunt ut labore et dolore magna aliqua. Ut enim ad minim veniam, quis nostrud exercitation ullamco laboris nisi ut aliquip ex ea commodo consequat.

This passage is so familiar to designers and developers that many can recite the first few words from memory. But almost nobody knows where it comes from or what it means — which is exactly why it works.

The 500-year origin: Cicero's De Finibus

Lorem ipsum is derived from De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum ("On the Ends of Good and Evil"), a philosophical work written by the Roman statesman and philosopher Marcus Tullius Cicero in 45 BC. Specifically, it comes from Book I, Section 10, Paragraph 32.

The original Latin text reads: "Neque porro quisquam est, qui dolorem ipsum quia dolor sit amet, consectetur, adipisci velit, sed quia non numquam eius modi tempora incidunt ut labore et dolore magnam aliquam quaerat voluptatem."

Translated to English, this means roughly: "Nor is there anyone who loves or pursues or desires to obtain pain of itself, because it is pain, but because occasionally circumstances occur in which toil and pain can procure him some great pleasure."

The key detail: The modern lorem ipsum is not a direct quote from Cicero. It's a scrambled, altered version — words rearranged and some removed — specifically designed to be unreadable as coherent Latin. This was done intentionally, so that the text wouldn't distract viewers from evaluating the design.

Who created the modern lorem ipsum?

The specific scrambled version used today was popularized in the 1960s by Letraset, a company that produced dry-transfer lettering sheets used by graphic designers before computers. Their sheets used the lorem ipsum passage as sample text, and it became the industry standard.

When desktop publishing software arrived in the 1980s — particularly Aldus PageMaker — lorem ipsum was included as the default placeholder text. From there, it spread to every design tool, word processor, and web platform in existence. By the time the internet made global sharing trivial, lorem ipsum was already too embedded to replace.

Why lorem ipsum works as placeholder text

The genius of lorem ipsum is that it fails to communicate meaning on purpose. This has three key design benefits:

  • It mimics real text visually. Lorem ipsum has a natural mix of word lengths, punctuation, and letter distribution that looks like real English prose. This lets designers evaluate typography, line length, and layout without being distracted by content.
  • It doesn't distract stakeholders. Real placeholder text (like "Insert headline here") invites readers to critique the words rather than the design. Lorem ipsum bypasses this — nobody can critique what they can't read.
  • It's universally understood as a placeholder. Any developer, designer, or client who sees lorem ipsum immediately understands the content is temporary. There's no ambiguity.

When NOT to use lorem ipsum

Despite its usefulness, there are situations where lorem ipsum is the wrong choice:

  • Usability testing: Real content reveals real usability problems. Testing with lorem ipsum hides issues with how users process actual information.
  • Accessibility evaluation: Screen readers and accessibility testing requires real text to assess reading flow, heading structure, and comprehension.
  • Client presentations (late stage): Showing a nearly finished design with lorem ipsum makes it look incomplete. Use real or realistic content for final approvals.
  • Content-dependent layouts: If your design depends on specific text lengths (a headline that must be under 40 characters, for example), lorem ipsum won't reveal whether the layout holds.

How many words is a standard lorem ipsum paragraph?

The standard lorem ipsum opening paragraph ("Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet...") contains approximately 69 words and 440 characters. Extended versions that include the full scrambled Cicero passage run to around 200 words.
