Frequently Asked Questions

How does the word counter work?

Our word counter splits your text by whitespace and counts each word in real time as you type. No button clicks needed — results update instantly.

Is there a word limit?

No. You can paste as much text as you want — entire essays, articles, or books. The counter handles any length instantly.

Does it count characters with or without spaces?

Both. We show character count with spaces and without spaces separately so you always have the right number for your platform.

Is my text saved or stored?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device and is never sent to any server.
