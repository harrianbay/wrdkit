Frequently Asked Questions

What are the two modes?

All Breaks mode removes every single line break, joining everything into one continuous block of text. Paragraph mode removes single line breaks but keeps double line breaks so your paragraphs stay intact.

Where do unwanted line breaks come from?

They commonly appear when copying text from PDFs, emails, Word documents, or e-books. Each line in the original document gets its own line break, which breaks the flow when pasted elsewhere.

Will it remove spaces too?

No. This tool only targets line breaks. Use the Remove Extra Spaces tool if you also need to clean up spacing.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
