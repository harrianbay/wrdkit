Frequently Asked Questions

What separators can I use?

You can separate repetitions with a new line, a space, a comma, or no separator at all. New line is best for lists, space for inline text, and none for joining characters directly.

How many times can I repeat text?

Up to 1000 repetitions. For very long text with many repeats the output can get large — your browser handles it all locally with no server involved.

What is text repeater used for?

Common uses include filling space for testing, creating lists, generating repeated patterns for design mockups, social media posts, and testing how interfaces handle long content.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
