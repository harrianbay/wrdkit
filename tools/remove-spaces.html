Frequently Asked Questions

What spaces does this tool remove?

It removes double and multiple spaces between words, leading spaces at the start of text, and trailing spaces at the end — leaving exactly one space between each word.

Where do extra spaces come from?

Extra spaces commonly appear when copying text from PDFs, Word documents, websites, or spreadsheets. They're invisible but can cause issues in code, databases, and formatting.

Does it remove spaces inside words?

No. It only removes extra spaces between words and at the start or end of the text. Spaces within words are left untouched.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
