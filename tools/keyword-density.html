Frequently Asked Questions

What is keyword density?

Keyword density is the percentage of times a keyword appears in your text compared to the total word count. A density of 1-2% is generally considered optimal for SEO without over-optimization.

What is a good keyword density for SEO?

Most SEO experts recommend keeping keyword density between 1% and 2%. Going above 3% can trigger keyword stuffing penalties from search engines like Google.

Does keyword density still matter for SEO?

It matters less than it used to but it's still a signal. Modern SEO focuses more on semantic relevance and topic coverage. However avoiding overuse of any single keyword remains important.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. All analysis runs locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
