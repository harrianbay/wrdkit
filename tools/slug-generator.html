Frequently Asked Questions

What is a URL slug?

A slug is the part of a URL that identifies a specific page. For example in "wrdkit.com/tools/word-counter" the slug is "word-counter". Slugs should be lowercase, hyphenated, and contain no special characters.

Why are slugs important for SEO?

Clean slugs help search engines understand what your page is about. A slug like "how-to-count-words" ranks better than "page?id=123" because it contains relevant keywords.

What does the slug generator do to my text?

It converts your text to lowercase, replaces spaces with hyphens, removes special characters and accents, and strips any extra hyphens. The result is a clean URL-ready slug.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
