Frequently Asked Questions

What is TikTok's bio character limit?

TikTok allows up to 80 characters in your profile bio. This is much shorter than Instagram's 150 character limit, so every word needs to count.

What should I put in my TikTok bio?

Your bio should clearly state who you are, what you post, and ideally include a call to action. With only 80 characters, be direct and specific — avoid vague phrases.

Do emojis count toward the limit?

Yes. Each emoji counts as one or two characters depending on the emoji. Keep this in mind if you use emojis as visual separators in your bio.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
