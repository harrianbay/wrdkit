Frequently Asked Questions

How does the line break adder work?

The tool adds a line break after every sentence ending with a period, exclamation mark, or question mark. This makes dense blocks of text easier to read.

When would I need to add line breaks?

Copying text from PDFs or documents often removes line breaks. This tool restores them, making the text readable again for emails, social posts, or documents.

Does it work with any language?

It works with any text that uses standard sentence-ending punctuation. Languages with different punctuation systems may not split correctly.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
