Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a character?

Every letter, number, space, punctuation mark, and symbol counts as one character. Spaces are included in the total character count but excluded from the "no spaces" count.

Why do I need to count characters?

Many platforms have character limits — Twitter allows 280, Instagram bios allow 150, and SMS messages are 160 characters. Knowing your count prevents truncation.

Does it count line breaks?

Yes. Each line break counts as one character in the total count, just like any other whitespace character.

Is there a character limit?

No limit. Paste entire documents, articles, or books. The counter handles any length instantly in your browser.
