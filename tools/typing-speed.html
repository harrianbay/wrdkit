Frequently Asked Questions

How is WPM calculated?

WPM (words per minute) is calculated by dividing the number of correctly typed characters by 5 (the standard word length), then dividing by the elapsed time in minutes.

What is a good typing speed?

Average typists do 40–60 WPM. Professional typists reach 70–100 WPM. Above 100 WPM is considered expert level.

Does accuracy matter more than speed?

Yes. Accuracy is more important than raw speed. A 95%+ accuracy rate should be your goal before pushing for higher WPM.

Can I paste text to cheat?

No — pasting is disabled during the test. The timer starts on your first keystroke and stops when you complete the text.
