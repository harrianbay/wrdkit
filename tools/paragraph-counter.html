Frequently Asked Questions

How does the paragraph counter work?

The counter detects paragraphs by looking for double line breaks between blocks of text. Each separated block counts as one paragraph.

Why count paragraphs?

Paragraph count helps with content structure. Blog posts typically need 6-10 paragraphs, academic essays have specific requirements, and SEO content benefits from well-spaced paragraphs for readability.

What if I only use single line breaks?

Single line breaks don't separate paragraphs — only double line breaks (pressing Enter twice) create a new paragraph. Single breaks count as the same paragraph.

Is my text saved or stored?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device and is never sent to any server.
