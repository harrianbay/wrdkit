Frequently Asked Questions

How secure are these passwords?

Passwords are generated using the browser's cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues). They are never sent to any server and exist only in your browser.

What is entropy and why does it matter?

Entropy measures how unpredictable a password is. Higher entropy means harder to crack. A 128-bit entropy password would take billions of years to brute force with current technology.

How long should my password be?

Security experts recommend at least 16 characters for important accounts. 20+ characters with mixed character types is considered very strong for 2026 threat models.

Are my passwords saved anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your passwords are never sent to any server, logged, or stored anywhere.
