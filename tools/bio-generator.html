Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a good social media bio?

A strong bio answers three questions instantly: who you are, what you do, and why someone should follow you. Keep it specific, use keywords relevant to your field, and include a call to action if space allows.

How long should a bio be?

Each platform has different limits. Twitter/X allows 160 characters, Instagram 150, TikTok 80, and LinkedIn 220. This tool generates bios optimized for each platform's limit.

Can I edit the generated bio?

Yes — the output is editable text. Generate a starting point then customize it with your own voice, specific achievements, or links.

Is my information stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing you type here is sent to any server.
