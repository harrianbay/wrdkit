Frequently Asked Questions

What is lorem ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is placeholder text used in design and publishing. It has roots in classical Latin literature from 45 BC and is used to fill space in mockups and prototypes without distracting from the design.

Why use lorem ipsum instead of real text?

Placeholder text lets designers focus on layout and typography without readers getting distracted by the content. It looks natural and fills space realistically unlike repeated dummy text.

Can I generate by words or sentences too?

Yes. Switch the type selector to Words or Sentences for more precise control over the amount of text generated.
