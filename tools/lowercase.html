Frequently Asked Questions

When should I use lowercase text?

Lowercase is used for normal body text, email addresses, URLs, code variables, and usernames. Many modern brands also use all-lowercase for a casual, approachable aesthetic.

Does it work with special characters?

Yes. The converter handles all Latin characters including accented letters like É, Ü, and Ñ, converting them to their lowercase equivalents.

Is there a text limit?

No limit. Paste entire documents and they convert instantly in your browser without any server processing.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
