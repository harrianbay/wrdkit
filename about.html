What is WrdKit

The free text tools platform built for writers, students, SEOs and developers

WrdKit is a free, browser-based text tools website with everything you need to write, analyze, convert, and optimize text online. Whether you're a blogger checking your word count for SEO, a student counting characters for a college essay, a social media manager counting Twitter characters, or a developer generating lorem ipsum placeholder text — WrdKit has the tool you need, available instantly, with no account required.

Every tool on WrdKit runs entirely in your browser. Your text is never sent to a server, never stored, never logged. When you close the tab, it's gone. This is the only way a text tool website should work.
