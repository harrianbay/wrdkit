Privacy Policy

WrdKit is built on one principle: your text is yours. We don't see it, store it, or share it. Every tool runs entirely in your browser.

Last updated: June 8, 2026
✅

The short version: we collect nothing

All WrdKit tools run 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never sent to our servers. We have no servers receiving your text. We don't store it, log it, sell it, or share it with anyone. Zero.

1

Who we are

WrdKit (wrdkit.com) is a free online text tools website. We provide browser-based text analysis tools including a free word counter, character counter, case converter, keyword density checker, reading time calculator, typing speed test, lorem ipsum generator, and 15+ additional tools. All tools are available for free with no account required.

For privacy questions, contact us at: privacy@wrdkit.com

2

Text and content you enter into our tools

We do not collect, receive, store, or process any text you enter into WrdKit tools.

Every tool on WrdKit executes entirely using JavaScript running in your web browser on your device. When you paste text into a WrdKit tool, that text stays on your device. It is not transmitted to any server. When you close or refresh the browser tab, it is gone permanently.

3

Information we do collect automatically

Like all websites, WrdKit receives standard web server access logs when you visit. This includes:

  • Your IP address (anonymized after 24 hours)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring URL
  • Pages visited and time on site
  • Date and time of visit

This data is used only for aggregate analytics and security purposes. It is never linked to your text content and never sold to third parties.

4

Cookies

WrdKit uses minimal cookies. We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking cookies, or any cookie that tracks your activity across other websites.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting the functionality of any WrdKit tool.

5

Analytics

WrdKit may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate site usage. Any analytics we use is configured to anonymize IP addresses, not share data with advertising networks, not track users across other websites, and respect Do Not Track browser settings.

We do not use Google Ads, Facebook Pixel, or any behavioral advertising tracking technology on WrdKit.

6

No account, no personal data

WrdKit requires no account to use any tool. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifying information. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to delete, correct, or export. This makes WrdKit GDPR and CCPA compliant by design.

7

Third-party services

WrdKit uses the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts — We load Inter font from Google Fonts. Google may log font requests. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Web hosting — Our hosting provider receives standard access logs as described in Section 3.
8

Children's privacy

WrdKit tools are appropriate for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. Since WrdKit collects no personal information from any user, children's data is equally protected by default.

9

GDPR rights (EEA users)

Since WrdKit holds no personal data about individual users, the practical exercise of most GDPR rights is not applicable — there is simply no data about you to access, delete, or export.

For any GDPR-related questions, contact us at privacy@wrdkit.com.

10

CCPA rights (California users)

WrdKit does not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for advertising purposes. Since we collect minimal information and no text content, your CCPA rights are protected by our architecture.

11

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Any changes will only make our privacy practices stronger, not weaker.

Questions? Email us: privacy@wrdkit.com
